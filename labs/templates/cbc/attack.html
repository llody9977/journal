The mechanism: XOR a single delta byte into ciphertext block 1, and the identical delta appears in block 2's decrypted plaintext, at the same offset, once the server decrypts it. No key needed — only knowledge of what character is already sitting at that offset.

This demo targets block 2, byte offset 13 — the character right after isadmin=, known value 0 — because it's a concrete, checkable way to see the flip land. Flip it to 1 and you've demonstrated the integrity failure end to end.

Choose any byte in block 1 or block 2 and a new character to flip it to. This only works because you (as the attacker) happen to know what character already sits at that position — you're not learning anything new, just rewriting something you already know.

Targeting block 1 flips a byte in the IV instead of a ciphertext block — watch what that does differently to block 2 in the result.
