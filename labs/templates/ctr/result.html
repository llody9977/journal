Why this happened

CTR mode XORs a keystream derived purely from the key and nonce onto the plaintext. Reuse that same key+nonce pair for a second message, and both ciphertexts were XORed with the identical keystream — so XORing the two ciphertexts together cancels the keystream out completely, leaving nothing but XOR(Pa, Pb). See the CTR section of the Field Journal for the same relationship demonstrated with real AES output, and why "reuse a nonce for one test run" is one of the most common real ways this failure ships to production.
