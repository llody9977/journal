3Recover a slice of one message from a guess about the other

Because the same keystream encrypted both, XOR(Ca, Cb) == XOR(Pa, Pb) — no key anywhere in that equation. If you already know (or can guess) a slice of one plaintext, XORing it into that same slice of XOR(Ca, Cb) cancels it out and leaves the corresponding slice of the other plaintext.

Try guessing a slice of message A to recover the corresponding slice of message B, or vice versa — the mechanism doesn't care which side you start from. A wrong guess doesn't error out; it just produces garbage, because the XOR happily runs regardless of whether the guess is correct.
